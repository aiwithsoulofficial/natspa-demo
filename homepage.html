About Natspa

Skin care from someone who's
been doing this longer than

"I believe great skin comes from understanding the whole person - not just the condition presenting on the surface."

Rennae Irvine founded Natspa Aesthetics with a conviction that skin health and whole-body wellness are inseparable. With over two decades treating every skin type and condition, her approach blends clinical precision with a deeply personal commitment to each client's wellbeing.

What makes Natspa rare in Essendon and Melbourne's north-west: this is not just a skin clinic. Dr Sean Arendse brings medical cosmetic expertise for injectables and facial contouring. Paul Sortino, Naturopath, works with clients whose skin challenges have roots in gut health, hormones, and lifestyle. You get the full picture.
