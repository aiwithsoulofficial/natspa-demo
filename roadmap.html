Critical Finding

The single fix that changes everything

Highest Priority
Your website is invisible to Google - right now

Your website currently has a setting that tells Google not to index any of your pages. This isn't a slow SEO problem. This is a switch that has been turned off. Every potential client searching for "skin clinic Essendon," "IPL Moonee Ponds," "dermapen Melbourne north," or "cosmetic physician Essendon" gets a result list - and Natspa isn't on it. Not at the bottom. Not on page two. Not anywhere.

This is almost certainly a WordPress setting called "Discourage search engines from indexing this site" that was left on after the site was built and never turned off. One checkbox. One click. And your entire site exists on Google again.

✓   WordPress Settings → Reading → Uncheck "Discourage search engines" → Save
Content Strategy

Your results belong on your website - not just Instagram stories

Two content changes that shift passive visitors into booked consultations.

🎥
Hero Content
Replace the walkthrough with before-and-afters

Right now your hero is a cinematic clinic walkthrough. It's beautiful - but it doesn't make anyone book. Before-and-after results make people book. They see themselves in your client. They think "that could be me."

Film on your phone. 15 seconds. Slow pan on the before. A couple of treatment cuts. Reveal the after. That's it. A $0 iPhone B&A outperforms a $5,000 walkthrough every time - because the walkthrough sells the clinic, and the B&A sells the result.

You already have a B&A highlight on Instagram with results content. That content exists. We just need it on your website where Google can see it and where visitors who arrive from search will find it immediately.
✎
Lead Capture
Capture the visitor who isn't ready to book today

Right now a visitor who isn't ready to book today has no way to stay connected. They land. They browse. They leave. And you lose them forever.

A 60-second skin concern quiz - like the one in this demo package - captures their details in exchange for a personalised recommendation. They feel helped, not sold. You get an email address. And you can nurture them with a simple 3-email sequence until they're ready to book.

The quiz in this demo took 2 minutes to build. It's branded to Natspa, it uses your real treatment pathways, and it outputs a personalised plan. That's what a lead capture system looks like when it works.
